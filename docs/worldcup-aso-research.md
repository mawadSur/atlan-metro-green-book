# Muslim Green Book — ASO First-Pass Research

## Competitor positioning from live search snippets

### Zabihah
- Claims global scale / largest halal guide.
- Search snippets emphasize halal restaurants, grocers, mosques, reviews, directions, next-prayer countdown.
- Atlanta snippets show large existing coverage, but that is exactly why Muslim Green Book should avoid “largest database” positioning and win on World Cup local accuracy.

### Muslim Pro
- Owns prayer times, Quran, Qibla, lifestyle utility.
- Very broad; not city-specific and not World Cup-specific.

### HalalTrip / travel guides
- Owns Muslim-friendly travel content, World Cup 2026 guides, prayer-space articles.
- Best earned-media target because Muslim Green Book is a live Atlanta-specific utility they can link.

### Muslim Directory
- Broad Islamic directory + Athan/Quran.
- Competes on “all-in-one Muslim app,” not match-day local trust.
